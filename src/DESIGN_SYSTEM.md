# Deen Zone Design System v2.0

## 🎨 Professional Color Palette

### Brand Identity (Modern Islamic)
- **Primary Emerald:** `#064e3b` (Sense of peace, growth, and sacredness)
- **Sacred Gold:** `#f59e0b` (Signifies divine guidance, reward, and value)
- **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF` (Clarity and purity for content surfaces)

### Functional Palette
- **Background:** `#F8FAFC` (Cool slate-50 for depth)
- **Text Primary:** `#0F172A` (Slate-900)
- **Text Secondary:** `#64748B` (Slate-500)

## ✍️ Typographic Mastery

- **Bengali (Primary):** `Hind Siliguri` (Clean, balanced, modern Naskh influence)
- **Display (English):** `Outfit` (Geometric, forward-thinking, approachable)
- **UI/Body:** `Inter` (Standard for maximum readability)
- **Scripture (Arabic):** `Amiri` (Elegant Naskh-style for Quranic text)

## 📏 Space & Structure
We utilize a base **4px grid** for precise alignment.
- **Micro-interactions:** 4-8px
- **Mobile Content:** 16px (`p-4`)
- **Desktop Content:** 24-32px (`p-6` to `p-8`)
- **Corner Radius:** `2.5rem` (40px) for main containers to create a soft, mobile-native app feel.

## 🍱 Component Architectures

### The "Floating Card"
- `bg-white`
- `border border-slate-100`
- `rounded-[2.5rem]`
- `shadow-sm` transitioning to `shadow-md` on hover.

### Tap-Sized Buttons
- Height: `56px` (Minimum for accessibility)
- Radius: `16px` (`rounded-2xl`)
- Animation: `active:scale-95 transition-all`
